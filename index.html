About Us

Welcome to Mama Maendeleo Resort

Located in the heart of Hindi, in the mainland West of Lamu County, Mama Maendeleo Resort is a newly established destination designed to offer a seamless blend of comfort and convenience. Whether visiting for business or leisure, our resort provides a strategic base less than 10 km from Kitangani Beach and within easy reach of Lamu Port & the LAPPSET transport network.
